Terms & Privacy

1. Purpose of Sadhana Card

Sadhana Card is a personal devotional spiritual accountability journal designed for practitioners of Krishna consciousness. It helps sadhakas log daily sadhana regulations—including bedtime, wake-up time, day rest, japa chanting, spiritual reading (Pathan), hearing lectures (Sravan), and practical service (Seva).

2. Pure Offline Privacy & Local Storage

Your devotional metrics, sadhaka name, and counselor name are stored 100% locally on your device using encrypted Room database and Jetpack DataStore preferences. No telemetry, third-party trackers, or cloud analytics are collected or transmitted across the network.

3. Data Ownership & Discretion

You have sole custody over your sadhana records. You may edit or delete daily records at any time. When sharing reports with your counselor, you retain complete authority over exported information.
